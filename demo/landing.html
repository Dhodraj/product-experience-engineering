The questions a dispatcher actually asks

We already have a tracker. Why another one?
A tracker says where a truck is. Consign says whether the run will make its times. That is a different question, and it is the one your customer rings about.
How long before it is useful?
It reads the sheet as it is. The first flagged run appears on the first day's runs, with nothing to migrate and nothing to re-key.
What happens to the depot that has no clean data?
A run with no planned window is shown as unwatched rather than guessed at. You will see exactly how much of your sheet it can read before you decide anything.
What happens after the trial?
The trial stops. Nothing is deleted, nothing is charged, and the sheet is still yours.
